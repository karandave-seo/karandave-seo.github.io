Case Study 01 • Agency Execution @ SHALIGRAM

Multi-Location Retail E-Commerce: Local SEO, Schema & Reporting

Client: Regional Jewellery Retailer (2 Branches, Ahmedabad) CMS: Odoo Stack: GBP, JSON-LD, GSC, GA4, Looker Studio
Challenge A retail jewellery e-commerce storefront operating two physical showrooms in North-West Ahmedabad needed stronger local map visibility, clearer entity signals for multi-branch search queries, and unified organic reporting.
Execution
  • Audited and optimized individual Google Business Profiles for both branch locations with consistent NAP alignment.
  • Authored and deployed custom LocalBusiness & Organization JSON-LD structured data on Odoo templates.
  • Built an automated Looker Studio dashboard blending GSC query data and GA4 engagement metrics.
Deliverables & Focus
  • Eliminated duplicate local entity ambiguity across branch landing pages.
  • Established clean Search Console indexing hygiene and weekly local discovery tracking.
  • Replaced manual spreadsheet reporting with live stakeholder dashboards.
Case Study 02 • Agency Execution @ SHALIGRAM

B2B Service Architecture: Technical Audits & Intent Clustering

Client: B2B Technology & Digital Services CMS: WordPress & Odoo Stack: Semrush, GSC, Screaming Frog / Technical Audits
Challenge High-intent commercial service pages suffered from overlapping keyword targeting, thin semantic heading structure, and crawl budget waste from unoptimized sitemap and robots rules.
Execution
  • Conducted full technical SEO audits covering XML sitemaps, robots.txt directives, canonical tags, and crawl depth.
  • Clustered commercial and informational keywords via Semrush to map distinct search intents to dedicated service URLs.
  • Restructured on-page HTML hierarchy (H1–H3), internal anchor links, and metadata for B2B decision-makers.
Deliverables & Focus
  • Resolved keyword cannibalization across core B2B service offerings.
  • Improved crawlability and indexation consistency for priority commercial pages.
  • Created repeatable on-page SEO templates for new case studies and solution pages.
Case Study 03 • Strategic Marketing Research

Integration of AI in Marketing

“Mapping Mechanical, Thinking, and Feeling AI across Meta, Amazon, Spotify, and Netflix.”

Core Framework Evaluated how platform business models dictate AI deployment—contrasting Meta's advertiser-ROI segmentation and Amazon's end-to-end retail conversion engine with Spotify and Netflix's churn-reducing recommendation loops.
Strategic Takeaway Sustainable marketing advantage comes from aligning specific AI tiers with core revenue mechanics while governing algorithmic bias and consumer privacy.

“Primary consumer research analyzing purchase drivers and adoption friction in urban Gujarat.”
