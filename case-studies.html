Case Study 04 • Consumer & Market Research

Factors Influencing Electric Two-Wheeler Purchases in Ahmedabad

Domain: Consumer Behavior & Market Research Location: Ahmedabad, Gujarat Tools: Survey Analysis, Excel / Google Sheets
Research Objective Identified the key economic, practical, and brand factors shaping buyer decisions around electric two-wheelers in Ahmedabad.
Key Decision Factors Evaluated upfront vs. running cost savings, real-world battery range, charging convenience, service reliability, and brand trust.
Strategic Takeaway Clear cost-of-ownership communication and practical range/charging confidence are essential to converting hesitant urban buyers.
